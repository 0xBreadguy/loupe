 HOW THIS IS COUNTED ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Time adds up each call's own length. Calls in a thread can overlap; counted once, they come to 72h on the clock.
 Recap's 'tools' hours are counted another way.

 305 calls were marked finished all at once, though they started at different times, most likely when a run stopped.
 When each really finished is not known, so their time is not counted and they are not among the longest: totals of
 time can only be too small. 3 more calls never ended and add no time.

 A line like "cd app && cargo test | tail" is one call, named for its main program, cargo: its time, failure and exit
 code are the whole line's. 57% of command lines ran two or more programs on the list. Programs are named from a list
 of 75 common ones; a command with none of them is "not on the list".

 Only program and tool names and exit codes are read. Numbers may be cached in your cache folder; nothing is sent.
